Contoh latihan resep makanan

Ingredients

Dough

  • 3/4 Cup warm milk
  • 2 1/4 teaspoon yeast
  • 1 egg + extra yolk
  • 1/4 cup butter
  • 3 cup bread flour

Filling

  • 2/3 dark brown sugar
  • 1/2 cup grated parmesan cheese
  • Ground cinnamon

Instructions

  1. Mix warm milk with yeast
  2. Melt butter and add to mixture
  3. Add flour and knead until smooth
  4. MASAK
